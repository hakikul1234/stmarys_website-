Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to contact the school?

Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. For urgent matters, you can call the main office. For non-urgent queries, email is preferred.

How long does it take to get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. However, complex queries may take 2-3 business days.

Can I visit the campus?

Yes! We welcome campus visits by appointment. Please contact the admissions office to schedule a tour. Visits are preferably arranged on Saturday mornings.

How do I schedule a meeting with the principal?

You can request a meeting through the office. Please provide at least 3 working days notice. The principal holds office hours on Wednesday afternoons.

Is there a parent-teacher meeting schedule?

Yes, parent-teacher meetings are held quarterly in September, December, March, and June. Dates are communicated well in advance.

How can I provide feedback or suggestions?

We value your feedback! You can submit suggestions through the contact form, email, or in person at the office. We review all feedback carefully.
