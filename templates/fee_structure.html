Payment Methods & Options

💳 Payment Methods

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience:

  • Demand Draft (DD)
  • Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS)
  • Online Payment Portal
  • Cash (at office)
  • Cheque

📅 Payment Schedule

Fees can be paid in the following installments:

  • Full Year: Due by July 31
  • Two Installments: July 31 & December 31
  • Quarterly: July 31, October 31, January 31, April 30
  • Monthly: Due on the 1st of every month

🏆 Scholarships & Discounts

Merit Scholarships: 10%-50% based on exam performance
Sibling Discount: 5% for second child, 10% for third
Financial Assistance: Available for deserving students
Early Payment Discount: 2% if paid in full by June 30

Important Information

Payment Policies

  • Late Payment: A fine of ₹500 per month will be charged for late payment of fees
  • Refund Policy: Admission fee is non-refundable. Other fees are refunded as per school policy
  • Leave Deduction: No deduction for student absence/leave except for medical emergencies
  • Fee Concession: Students with financial difficulties can apply for fee concession with proper documentation
  • Defaulters: Students with pending dues cannot appear in exams or receive certificates
  • Annual Revision: Fees are subject to revision on an annual basis
