Frequently Asked Questions

What is the eligibility criteria?

We accept applications from students across all academic levels (Class I to XII). There is no age bar, and applications from all religions, castes, and backgrounds are encouraged.

When can I submit my application?

Applications are open year-round. However, for the academic year 2024-25, the last date for submission is June 30, 2024. Early submission is recommended.

Is there an entrance exam?

Yes, candidates are required to appear for an entrance assessment which evaluates academic knowledge and aptitude. This helps us place students in appropriate academic levels.

What are the admission fees?

Admission fees vary by class level. Please refer to our Fee Structure page or contact the admissions office for detailed information about fees for your chosen class.

When will I receive the admission result?

Results are typically announced within 2-3 weeks of the entrance exam. Parents will be notified via email and phone. Results are also displayed on our notice board.

Is there any scholarship available?

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships for deserving students. Scholarships are awarded based on entrance exam performance and family income. Contact us for details.
